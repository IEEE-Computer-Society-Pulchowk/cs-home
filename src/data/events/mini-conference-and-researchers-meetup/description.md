**Nepal Computer Science Researchers Meetup and Mini-Conference**

*A day-long conference by IEEE Computer Society, Pulchowk Student Branch Chapter*

The Nepal Computer Science Researchers Meetup and Mini-Conference brings together students interested in research and experienced researchers for a day of paper and poster presentations, talks from professionals, and networking. It provides a platform for students to present their work before a panel of judges, exchange ideas with researchers, and gain valuable insights into research and its various avenues.
