**SITM Mini Research Conference — August 15, 2026**

The program culminates in a mini research conference on **August 15, 2026**, where selected participants present their papers before a panel of judges in a simulated academic conference experience.

- **Presenters:** the top **7–10** research papers shortlisted from the final submission.
- **Prize pool:** **NPR 10,000**, awarded to the strongest papers based on both the quality of the paper and the presentation.
- **Venue:** announced closer to the date.
