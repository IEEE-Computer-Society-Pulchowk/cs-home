We are happy to announce a major milestone for our community: the launch of the **IEEE Computer Society Student Branch Chapter Pulchowk website**.

### What’s New?

The website now has a cleaner home for updates, stories, and student activities. It is built to make it easier to find the latest information without making things feel crowded. Here is what you can expect:

- **Events and activities:** Stay updated on workshops, talks, and competitions.
- **Stories and updates:** Read simple announcements and highlights from the chapter.
- **Member space:** Find quick information that helps members stay connected.
- **Project highlights:** See the work students are building and sharing.

### Built on Earlier Work

This launch continues from the earlier IEEE PSB website work and extends it into the Computer Society chapter site. It builds on the foundation created by @{Animesh-Dhakal}'s work and carries it forward with a simpler and more focused presentation.

### Why It Matters

The goal is not just to put information online, but to make it easier for students to stay informed, discover what is happening, and feel part of the chapter.

We hope this update helps more people discover what the chapter is doing and what is coming next.
